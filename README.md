## Entry

> I bought the dip. The dip dipped. I bought that dip too. My portfolio is now a museum of falling knives.

One line, 104 characters, about crypto and trading life. The setup is every
trader's habit (buying the dip), the turn is the dip dipping again, and the
payoff is the bag-holder's museum of falling knives.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the joke as a literal to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
and checks the 1–200 character gate before writing. No network, no browser,
no rendering — the program is self-contained.
